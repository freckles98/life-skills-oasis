Team Awarded Child Protector Ambassador Certificates

14 July 2026

The Life Skills Oasis team was recently awarded certificates as trained Child Protector Ambassadors, presented by the local police. Our team members are now fully trained and ready to serve, standing as frontline protectors for the vulnerable children in our community.

This recognition reflects our ongoing commitment to keeping the children of the Oasis safe, and strengthens the partnership between Life Skills Oasis and local law enforcement in safeguarding the next generation.

Our New Building Is Coming Along Nicely

Since December 2025

The Oasis's new building has been coming along nicely since December 2025. What was previously a simple shack has now been transformed into a two-story building! Construction is still in progress and an end date isn't clear yet, but the bottom floor is already functional and working well for our ongoing Oasis support programs.

The children have wasted no time making the new space their own — already playing, learning, and enjoying meals together on the finished ground floor while work on the upper story continues.

To protect their privacy and safety, the faces of the children in these photos have been blurred.
